# Atlas Card

> Atlas is a technology company (not a bank) that offers an invite-only luxury lifestyle membership built around a premium steel charge card. Atlas Card is issued by Lead Bank. Membership bundles a CNC-milled metal charge card with no preset spending limit, a concierge-driven mobile app, curated dining reservations, luxury hotel bookings, sold-out event access, points-based rewards, and thousands of dollars in annual travel and lifestyle credits.

This is a company profile in the API Evangelist network. Atlas Card publishes no public developer API; the only machine-discoverable surface is a minimal OIDC authorization endpoint used by its own consumer app.
